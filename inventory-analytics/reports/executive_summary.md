## Executive Assessment
The retail network generated **$31.95M** in revenue at a **32.40%** gross margin over the 24-month window, but working capital efficiency is constrained by a structural imbalance: **50.0%** of SKUs (250 products) are overstocked and **$1.40M** is locked in dead stock, while **114 SKUs** (including 4 of the top 5 revenue-generating Electronics items) sit at Low or Critical stock levels and require immediate replenishment of **8,829 units**.
